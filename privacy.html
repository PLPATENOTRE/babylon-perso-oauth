Privacy policy

babylon-perso is a private application used by a single person, its owner, on their own Google accounts.

  • Data accessed: Gmail messages (read-only) and drafts (create) of the owner's accounts.
  • Use: only to answer the owner's own requests (search, summarize, prepare drafts). No email is ever sent by the application.
  • Storage: OAuth tokens and data stay on the owner's private server. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or model training.
  • Limited Use: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
